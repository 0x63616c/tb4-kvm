# PCBWay pre-quote engineering inquiry

Status: `DRAFT — do not submit or authorize fabrication without owner approval`.

PCBWay's public pages establish plausible generic capability, not acceptance of this RF coupon. PCBWay says its impedance calculator is approximate and that final geometry/construction must be calculated by the factory; it also warns that glass style, resin content and material availability matter above 10 Gb/s. See [impedance calculator](https://www.pcbway.com/pcb_prototype/impedance_calculator.html) and [impedance-control guidance](https://www.pcbway.com/pcb_prototype/Impedance_Control.html).

## Vendor inquiry text

**Subject:** Pre-quote engineering confirmation — controlled-impedance RF mux coupon

> We are preparing a measurement-only RF mux coupon. This inquiry is for engineering confirmation before fabrication; it is not authorization to manufacture.
>
> Please provide a dated written response tied to the quote number covering:
>
> 1. Your proposed frozen layer stack: exact laminate/prepreg manufacturer and grade, glass style, resin content, pressed dielectric thickness and tolerance, Dk/Df source/test method/frequency, copper foil type/roughness, and starting/finished copper thickness.
> 2. Final production trace width/spacing for every controlled-differential structure: reference plane, dielectric height, soldermask treatment, solver inputs and guaranteed tolerance.
> 3. Whether a representative impedance coupon will be fabricated on every production panel: coupon design/ownership, location, test method, sampling, calibration, limits, raw TDR delivery, and panel/lot mapping.
> 4. Available order-specific records: as-built stack-up, CoC, material/copper lot certificates, lot/date code, impedance report, microsection/copper-thickness report, and redacted traveler or equivalent traceability.
> 5. Written DFM review of mux footprint, launches, vias, reference planes, calibration/de-embedding structures and panelization. No CAM, stack-up, material, trace-geometry or coupon change may occur without written approval.
> 6. Job-specific via, plating, surface-finish, soldermask, registration, finished-hole and board-thickness limits.
>
> Identify any unavailable, sampled-only or extra-cost request. Generic website capability statements are not acceptance evidence for this job.
